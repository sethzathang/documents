Frequently asked

Do I need an account to play?
No. Tap “Play as Guest” — your progress is saved on your device.

I lost connection during a game. What happens?
Tenhouse pauses the table and gives you a short window to reconnect right where you left off.

How do I play with friends?
Create a private room and share the room code, or have them enter it to join your table.

Are coins real money?
No. Coins are a virtual, in-game score only — they can’t be purchased or exchanged for real money.
