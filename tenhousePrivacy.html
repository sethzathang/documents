Privacy Policy for Tenhouse

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Tenhouse ("the app", "we", "us") is a real-time multiplayer card game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No sign-up form. The app creates an anonymous account for you automatically. We do not ask for your email address, password, or phone number.
  • No advertising or behavioral profiling. We do not sell your data or use it to target ads.
  • You choose a display name, avatar, and region (and, for some regions, a state or subdivision) during setup.
  • Your profile and game progression are stored in our cloud database so they sync across sessions and devices tied to your account.
  • Friends, invites, and online status use your profile information as described below.
  • We collect limited crash and diagnostic data to find and fix bugs (see below). This is not used for advertising.
  • Active match data on our game server is held in memory only for the duration of a game session.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Display name — your first and last name as shown in the app. You can change these in your profile. They are stored in our database and shown to other players during games and in social features.
  • Avatar — one of a fixed set of built-in cartoon avatars you select. This is a preset choice, not an uploaded image or photo.
  • Region and state — the country or region (and, where applicable, state or subdivision) you select during setup. This is information you choose; we do not collect GPS or precise location data.

Account and profile data

When you first open the app, we create an anonymous account for you through our authentication service. No email or password is required. Your account is linked to:

  • A randomly assigned Profile ID (shown as a short numeric code, e.g. 1234) that other players can use to send you friend requests.
  • Game progression — experience points (XP), level, games played, games won, and related stats, stored in our database and synced with the app.
  • Session credentials — access and refresh tokens stored on your device so you stay signed in.

Friends and social features

If you use friends or private-room features, we store and process:

  • Friend requests and friendships — who you have sent requests to, received requests from, or are friends with.
  • Room invites — invitations you send or receive to join a private game.
  • Online presence — whether you appear online to friends, based on your connection to our server (not GPS).

Your Profile ID, display name, and avatar are visible to friends and to players you interact with through these features.

Crash and diagnostic data

To keep the app stable, we use a third-party crash-reporting service that automatically collects information when the app crashes or encounters certain errors. This may include:

  • Crash reports — stack traces and error details needed to diagnose what went wrong.
  • Device and app information — such as app version, operating system version, and device model.
  • Basic usage signals — limited diagnostic events (for example, app opens and in-app actions leading up to a crash) to help us understand context around a failure. We use these only to improve reliability, not for advertising or profiling.

Crash and diagnostic data is not linked to your display name or Profile ID in a way that is shown to other players. It is tied to an anonymous installation identifier assigned by the reporting service.

Information stored on your device

The app also keeps the following locally on your device:

  • Session tokens — so you do not have to sign in again each time you open the app.
  • Cached profile and settings — for faster loading and offline viewing of some screens.

You can remove locally stored data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Cloud profile data remains until you delete your account (see below).

Information shared during gameplay

When you join a match, your display name, avatar, Profile ID, and progression stats (such as XP, level, and win record) are sent to our server and shown to the other players at your table.

Technical information

  • When your device connects to our game server, our hosting provider processes standard connection information such as your IP address as a normal part of delivering the service. We do not use this to identify you or build a profile.
  • The app sends its app version and platform (Android or iOS) to our server to check whether an update is required.

Information We Do Not Collect

To be explicit, Tenhouse does not collect or access:

  • Your email address, password, or phone number
  • Your GPS location or precise geolocation
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files
  • Advertising identifiers for targeted ads
  • Data for behavioral advertising or cross-app tracking

On Android, the app requests only the INTERNET permission, which is needed to connect to our servers and send crash reports.

How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to:

  • Create and maintain your anonymous account and profile
  • Run multiplayer matches and show players to one another during a game
  • Power friends, room invites, and online presence
  • Save and sync your profile, avatar, region, and progression between sessions
  • Prompt you to update the app when a new version is required
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve app stability and performance
  • Enforce our Terms and keep the service safe

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

How Information Is Shared

  • With other players: your display name, avatar, Profile ID, and in-game stats are visible to opponents during a match and to friends in social features, as described above.
  • With service providers: we use third-party cloud infrastructure to provide anonymous authentication and to store profile, friends, and related data in a hosted database. These providers process data on our behalf under their terms and privacy policies.
  • With our hosting provider: our game server runs on a third-party cloud hosting provider, which processes connection data on our behalf to host real-time gameplay.
  • With our crash-reporting provider: we use a third-party service to collect and analyze crash and diagnostic data on our behalf. Reports are used only to operate and improve the app.
  • App distribution: the app is distributed through the Google Play Store and Apple App Store, which have their own privacy practices.

We do not share your information with other third parties for their own marketing purposes, and we never sell it.

Data Retention

  • Cloud profile: your profile, progression, friends, and related account data are kept in our database until you delete your account in Settings or we remove it for a violation of our Terms.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: retained by our crash-reporting provider for as long as needed to investigate issues and improve the app, then deleted or aggregated according to their retention practices.
  • Game server: active match and room data on the game server is held in memory only for the duration of a session. It is not written to a long-term match-history database and is discarded when the match ends or the server restarts.
  • On your device: session tokens and cached data stay on your device until you clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or delete your account.

Children's Privacy

Tenhouse is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can change your display name, avatar, and region at any time within the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings. This permanently deletes your cloud profile and associated data, including friends and progression, subject to our data processors completing deletion.
  • You can remove locally stored data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling also stops further crash reports from that installation.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights regarding personal data. Contact us below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We use secure, encrypted connections between the app and our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and collect only what we need to run the service.

International Users

The app may be used from anywhere. Our servers, database, hosting providers, and crash-reporting provider may process data in the United States and other countries where they operate. By using the app you understand that your data is processed as described in this policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, noted in the app.
