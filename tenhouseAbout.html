About Tenhouse

Tenhouse is a social card game — and a digital home for Dawlcuh, a traditional Burmese–Chin card game. It's the kind of fast, friendly game that families and friends gather around the table to play.

Dawlcuh is the game's original Burmese–Chin name. Tenhouse was built to help preserve that heritage and share it beyond the living room — to keep the game alive for the next generation and let players everywhere enjoy it, whether they grew up playing it or are dealing their first hand.

Every table is a small way of carrying a tradition forward. Thank you for being part of it.
